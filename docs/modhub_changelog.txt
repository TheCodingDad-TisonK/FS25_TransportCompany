v1.0.0.0

- Initial release
- Freight contracts generated from the loading and unloading stations already
  present on your map, priced against the live economy
- Bulk freight measured in liters, pallet freight in pallets
- Haul a contract yourself, or hand the route to a hired driver via the base
  game AI; a job you accepted can still be handed over later
- Over-delivery rolls onto your next open job instead of being wasted
- Per-truck books: distance, fuel valued at the live economy price, revenue,
  driver wages and completed jobs
- Company ledger with a history of finished and expired contracts
- In-game settings: board size, deadline, driver wage share, notifications
- Multiplayer support, server-authoritative
- English and German
- Works on any map, no preparation required
